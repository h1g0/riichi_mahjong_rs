[Illustration AC](https://www.ac-illust.com/):

- The mahjong tile images under `tiles/` are adapted in part from
  [Illustration AC asset 1077814](https://www.ac-illust.com/main/detail.php?id=1077814).
- The score-stick images under `sticks/` are adapted in part from
  [Illustration AC asset 26784794](https://www.ac-illust.com/main/detail.php?id=26784794).
